   ...Synopsis...
   On Saturday, a broad upper trough will develop into Ontario, with a
   leading speed max moving from the northern Plains across the Great
   Lakes. Meanwhile, an upper high will be centered over the Great
   Basin, with relatively high midlevel heights extending into the
   Southeast.

   At the surface, a high pressure center will move from MI into the
   Northeast, with the surface ridge extending southwestward into the
   southern High Plains. Northeasterly surface winds will thus relegate
   the more robust moisture/dewpoints to the Gulf Coast states.

   Isolated thunderstorms may develop during the day over the Upper
   Midwest in association with the shortwave trough, as temperatures
   aloft cool. Moisture and instability will be minimal, but weak
   elevated instability should support at least isolated lightning
   flashes.

   Otherwise, scattered thunderstorms will affect much of the Gulf
   Coast and extending into the Carolinas as the air mass will become
   marginally unstable with heating. Weak shear will further preclude
   severe possibilities.
