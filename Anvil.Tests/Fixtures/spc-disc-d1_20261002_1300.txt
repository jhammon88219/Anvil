   ...NO SEVERE THUNDERSTORM AREAS FORECAST...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Severe thunderstorm potential is low today and tonight.

   ...Mid-Atlantic States/Cumberland Plateau...
   A progressive stream of westerlies will continue to reside over the
   northern third of the CONUS through tonight. An extensive
   northeast/southwest-oriented cold front will continue
   east-southeastward, reaching the coastal Northeast/Mid-Atlantic by
   this evening, while otherwise crossing the Tennessee Valley and
   lower Mississippi Valley. Scattered showers/thunderstorms will
   accompany the front, while also being common across coastal areas of
   the Gulf Coast and Southeast. 

   As the cold front approaches the coastal Mid-Atlantic/Delmarva later
   this evening, semi-strong wind profiles (35+ kt effective shear)
   will exist regionally, and to a lesser extent southwestward toward
   the Appalachians and Cumberland Plateau. A couple of stronger storms
   with gusty winds could materialize this afternoon near/ahead of the
   front in these regions provided sufficient insolation/modest
   destabilization. However, moist-profile/subsidence-related poor
   lapse rates aloft should preclude organized severe storms.
