   ...SUMMARY...
   Strong to locally severe thunderstorms may occur from the
   south-central Plains into parts of the Midwest this afternoon and
   evening, with at least isolated damaging wind gusts possible.

   ...Central Plains into Midwest...
   Moderately fast zonal flow remains across the northern US today,
   with the primary surface boundary extending from the TX Panhandle
   east-northeastward into parts of KS/MO/IL.  This boundary will be
   enhanced by daytime differential heating, and will provide the focus
   for scattered thunderstorms along its length.  Most of this activity
   will be relatively disorganized, but in an environment of moderate
   CAPE and steep low-level lapse rates favorable for occasional
   strong/severe downdrafts.

   One area of greater concern extends across northern MO into
   west-central IL.  Morning CAM solutions suggest higher confidence in
   storm coverage in this area, and forecast soundings show sufficient
   deep-layer shear for organized multicell or occasional supercell
   structures.  However, much of this activity may occur on the north
   side of the baroclinic zone with slightly weaker support for intense
   downburst winds.  Warm temperatures aloft should limit hail concern.
   Therefore, have opted to maintain the MRGL risk category and
   re-evaluate at the 20z update.
